# 📦 Projects
- *2026.06 - 2027.06*, Research on Multidimensional Adaptive Personalized Federated Visual Defect Detection for Heterogeneous Data in Industrial Production Lines, *Hunan Provincial College Students' Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program*, Project Leader (Rank 1).
  - **Description**: To address the data silos in intelligent manufacturing caused by commercial privacy restrictions on multi-line defect data, as well as the data distribution shifts induced by differences in cameras, lighting, and backgrounds, a single global quality inspection model struggles to fit the true distribution of each production line. This leads to challenges such as weak model generalization, high defect miss detection rates, and significant accuracy degradation in cross-line deployment. This project aims to develop a personalized federated learning framework for visual defect detection. Under the premise of strictly protecting commercial data privacy and without sharing raw product images, the framework decouples line-agnostic defect semantics from local environmental style features through a multi-dimensional adaptive aggregation mechanism. Combined with dynamic representation alignment to further enhance the discriminative power of defect features, it adaptively customizes highly accurate and robust visual quality inspection models for each production line.
